Online Retail Data Engineering and Business Intelligence

An end-to-end ETL pipeline that turns 541,909 raw retail transactions into a star-schema data warehouse and an interactive Power BI dashboard. Python handles preprocessing (duplicates, missing values, invalid quantities and prices), Pentaho Data Integration runs four transformations (dim_product, dim_customer, dim_time, fact_sales) orchestrated by a single job, and MySQL stores the staging and warehouse tables.

The Power BI dashboard analyzes £8.72M in revenue across 4,338 customers and 3,665 products, covering sales trends, customer behavior, and product performance. It shows that the UK accounts for over 92% of transactions, that sales peak in October and November, and that returning customers outnumber new ones every month.

Home Credit Default Risk: Big Data Processing

A group case study on predicting loan default risk with a big data stack. Raw Home Credit data from Kaggle was stored in HDFS (Cloudera Quickstart VM) and processed with PySpark: cleaning and validation of the application and previous-application tables, duplicate and invalid-value removal, aggregation, and feature engineering such as credit-to-income ratios and historical credit behavior. A Random Forest classifier then predicts which customers are likely to default.

The model reached an AUC of 0.74, and feature importance showed that external credit scores (EXT_SOURCE_3 and EXT_SOURCE_2), employment duration, and age were the strongest predictors. The report also covers the big data architecture, a public cloud vs. on-premise comparison, and limitations such as class imbalance and running Spark in local mode instead of on a physical cluster.

Linear Regression and Greedy Join Order Optimization

A lightweight framework that predicts query cost and picks a better join order for hash join queries, using only structural query features and no internal DBMS statistics. It was built on 720 query executions from the Classicmodels MySQL database with 17 features. A Linear Regression model on log-transformed execution time estimates cost, and a Greedy Algorithm selects the lowest predicted-cost join order.

The model achieved an R² of 87.45% (RMSE 0.3044 ms, MAE 0.2206 ms) and was evaluated on 128 join-order scenarios. The greedy order ran faster in 60.2% of scenarios, with an average speedup of 2.87% and a maximum of 39.62%. Gains were largest for five-table joins (9.21% on average).

Terrascent: Eco-Friendly Perfume Brand and Mobile Prototype

A group final project for the Creativity and Innovation course (BINUS University, even semester 2024/2025) with four teammates. Terrascent is a perfume brand concept built around reusable wooden bottles, refill pouches, a scent-matching quiz for personalization, and a return-bottle loyalty system. It targets the packaging waste of an industry that ships more than 120 billion cosmetic and fragrance packaging units every year.

The report covers two user personas, an empathy map, a perceptual map that places Terrascent in the high-sustainability, affordable quadrant, and the revenue streams and cost structure. The Figma prototype walks through the landing page, home, product list, the Find Your Scent quiz, return bottles, cart and checkout, order status, order tracking, and favorites.
